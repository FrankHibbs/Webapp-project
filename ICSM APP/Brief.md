Gameplan turns an athlete’s goals into a sport-specific training plan. It’s for athletes who want targeted guidance tailored to their sport, skill level, and ambitions. The user answers a set of questions, and the app analyzes their responses to create a personalized weekly schedule and a multiweek training calendar, with daily plans detailing drills, exercises, and recommended durations or sets. Athletes can check off completed sessions, track progress, view short drill instructions or videos, and receive reminders. Gameplan won’t connect to wearables or fitness trackers or include social networking features. Its look and feel will be athletic, modern, and organized, using a dark navy and white palette with bright orange buttons and highlights, plus clean typography and bold, easy-to-scan headings. The project will include whiteboard wireframes of two to four key screens, with arrows showing how users move through the app.
